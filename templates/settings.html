Pegasus

Pegasus is two apps for one practice: contact with yourself. Long Track and Ledger each build that contact a different way. Neither replaces the other. Long Track does not need Ledger. Ledger is a separate record of thoughts and emotions.

The Long Track cards draw connection to other people, to work, and to the living world. Then they say one thing is required for any of that to be possible: connection to yourself. Without it, what you send toward another person is marked return to sender. Work is still on the calendar, and it looks worse. The tree is still there, and you cannot draw it. The arrow does not arrive, because it never left a person in contact with themselves.

That is a hypothesis of this practice. Self-connection is treated as a requirement of any other connection. Practice it, and a deeper connection with your world becomes possible. Connection is the material love is built from. Self-connection is the material self-love is built from.

Self-connection, in this suite, is contact with core values: the things that, named honestly, still claim you. Ledger practices that contact by attention to the inner world: body, emotion, urge, thought. Listening is the practice. The record is of what is happening inside. Long Track practices it indirectly, through ownership. A responsibility rings true when it points at a value. Naming what is yours, what you owe it, and what happened is how you stay in contact with the value when you do not feel like it — especially when it is hard to face.

The shape of this suite is acceptance and commitment work, kept as two records. Acceptance, here, is contact with inner experience without requiring that it change first. That is Ledger. Committed action is behavior chosen because of what you value, including when you do not feel like it. That is ownership in Long Track. Act required is the named commitment. An act records that you did it. A miss records that you did not. Repair names what now has to be made right. None of that waits on feeling ready.

The two stay separate on purpose. Ledger does not decide what you owe. Long Track does not keep the emotion or the journal text. When they are connected, a moment here can become a plain act in Long Track, by a rule you set there, or wait in Long Track’s Inbox until you say whether it touched those claims. The interpretation stays yours.

  1. Practice self-connection.
  2. A deeper connection with your world becomes possible.
  3. Connection is the material love is built from.
  4. Self-connection is the material self-love is built from.

There is no score for this. The record is of your truth. Contact with what is true now is enough.
